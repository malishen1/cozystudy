Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Cozy Study: Focus Timer does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Data on your device

Your timer settings and study minutes are saved only on your iPhone, in the app's local storage. They never leave your device and are deleted when you delete the app.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules local reminders on your phone when a focus or break block ends. No data is sent to a server.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
